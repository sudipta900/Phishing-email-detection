Phishing Indicators

Learn to identify phishing emails with these key warning signs.

⚠

Urgency & Fear Tactics

Phrases like "Act Now", "Account Suspended", "Immediate Action Required" create panic to bypass rational thinking.

URGENT 24 hours suspended expires today
🔗

Suspicious URLs

Links with IP addresses, excessive subdomains, misspelled brand names, or URL shorteners are major red flags.

192.168.1.1/login paypa1.com bit.ly/...
🏢

Brand Impersonation

Attackers mimic trusted brands like PayPal, Apple, Microsoft, or banks to gain trust and steal credentials.

PayPal Security Apple Support Microsoft Team
🔑

Sensitive Data Requests

Legitimate services never ask for passwords, SSNs, credit card numbers, or PINs via email.

password credit card SSN PIN
🎁

Too-Good-To-Be-True Offers

Lottery wins, prize claims, free gifts, or unexpected inheritances are classic social engineering lures.

winner free gift million dollars
✍

Poor Grammar & Formatting

Excessive caps, typos, strange punctuation!!!!, and odd formatting can indicate automated or non-native phishing attempts.

ALL CAPS !!!!!! spelling errors
