Urgency and fear tactics

Messages that pressure users to act immediately are a common social engineering tactic.

urgent 24 hours suspended immediate action
URL

Suspicious links

Watch for misspelled domains, shortened links, IP-based URLs, and overly nested subdomains.

paypa1.com bit.ly 192.168.1.1
ID

Brand impersonation

Attackers often copy the tone and appearance of trusted companies to make the email feel legitimate.

bank alert apple support microsoft team
KEY

Credential requests

Any direct request for passwords, card numbers, one-time codes, or PINs should be treated carefully.

password PIN verify account
GIFT

Unexpected offers

Prize claims, giveaways, and inherited wealth narratives are still effective bait because they create excitement.

winner free gift claim reward
TXT

Poor writing signals

Typos, excessive punctuation, and odd formatting are weak signals alone, but useful in combination.
